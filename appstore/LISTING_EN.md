## Description (max 4000)
Stop throwing money in the bin. MyFridge shows you what's in your fridge and reminds you before it goes bad.

SCAN YOUR RECEIPT
Photograph your grocery receipt and MyFridge adds your items for you, sorted by category. Works with English and French receipts from Canadian stores.

NEVER MISS AN EXPIRY DATE
See at a glance what expires soon. Get a reminder a few days before, or one friendly summary each day. You choose.

YOUR FRIDGE ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Add the widget to see what to eat first, and tick items off without opening the app.

BUILT FOR CANADA
Understands Canadian and Quebec grocery receipts and products, from lait and yogourt to poutine, in English and French.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your items stay on your iPhone. Sign in with Apple, no ads, no tracking.

FREE TO START
Scan one receipt per month for free, and add items by hand as often as you like.

MYFRIDGE PREMIUM (optional)
Unlimited receipt scans with an auto-renewing subscription: $2.99 CAD per month or $29.99 CAD per year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.
