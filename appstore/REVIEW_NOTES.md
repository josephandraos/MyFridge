# Notes for App Review (paste into App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes)

MyFridge helps people reduce food waste: you scan a grocery receipt (or add items by hand), see expiry dates, and get reminders before food expires.

HOW TO TEST
1. Sign in with Apple (no other account is needed).
2. Tap "Add Manually" to add an item, or tap "Scan Receipt" and point the camera at any printed grocery receipt. If no receipt is available, a sample receipt image is attached to this submission.
3. Open the items list from the fridge icon to see items grouped by category with expiry status.
4. Settings > Notifications to configure reminders. A "Send Test Notification" button is provided.
5. Add the MyFridge widget to the home screen to see items expiring soon.

SUBSCRIPTION
The free tier includes one receipt scan per month. "MyFridge Premium" (monthly or yearly, auto-renewing) removes the limit. Open the profile (person icon) or scan a second receipt to see the paywall.

PRIVACY
All data stays on the device. The app makes no network requests of its own and has no analytics or advertising.

LANGUAGES
English and French (Canada). The app follows the device language.
